      A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

      Yours, all the way.

      Your account can only read its own rides; accounts are separated in storage and personal API responses are not publicly cached. The site operator must protect the server and its database. We import only the Strava activities list and cycling tracks from your archive, not the separate profile, social, health, or privacy-zone files.

      To plan a route, the app sends your chosen departure, stops, and arrival points to its bicycle-routing provider. Place search sends your search text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service; current weather for the map centre and route, sampled route elevations, and sunset times are requested from Open-Meteo; and map tiles reveal the displayed map area to OpenStreetMap. Greenway paths are mapped OpenStreetMap bicycle routes, so completeness depends on the community map data. Use of these external services is optional to your account but needed for those features.

      Connect Strava to sync future rides, or import an archive snapshot. You can also sync manually any time.

      Route estimates use your median moving speed from recent rides. They’re a guide, not a promise—and don’t include stops or traffic signals.
